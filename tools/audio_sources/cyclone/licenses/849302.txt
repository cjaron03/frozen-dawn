WNW Wind and Snow on Holehead Hill
Overall rating (15 ratings)
Sandy-Ogilvie
March 31st, 2026
Follow
Soundscapes
>
Nature
Recorded in a small gap in a drystone dyke wall which ran parallel with the wind.  Microphones set up quickly just before a sudden onset of constant and high wind coming in from the west-northwest bringing snow and layers and textures of sound whipping across the bare landscape.  A random fact I've found out since recording that day- "A record was set in the United Kingdom, with a half-hour power record of 23,880 MW for wind generation achieved on March 25, 2026, between 13:30-14:00." Which was exactly the time I decided it was getting too cold to stay.
Unfortunately some creaking plastic sounds from my blimp which is getting sent away for repair.
25/03/26, 11:07
