# Rimebound audio sources

All Rimebound audio in this directory is original procedural synthesis generated by
`tools/generate_rimebound_sounds.py`. It uses no recorded or third-party source audio.

The second encounter-polish set is original procedural synthesis generated by
`tools/generate_rimebound_sound_polish.py`. It also uses no recorded or third-party audio.

The palette is limited to filtered noise, low ground resonance, irregular ice impulses,
and non-vocal frequency sweeps. This keeps it distinct from the Heart, Maeve, Thae Iven,
and ordinary Frostbitten sound vocabularies.
