# Remnant audio sources

The Remnant's recorded source material is released under Creative Commons Zero
(`CC0 1.0`). Frozen Dawn ships processed derivatives made by
`tools/generate_remnant_sounds.sh`.

- `ghost_wails_cc0.mp3`
  - **Ghost wails** by colorsCrimsonTears
  - https://freesound.org/s/556419/
  - CC0 1.0
- `spirit_breathing_cc0.mp3`
  - **Scary ghost/spirit breathing** by 4_and_a_half_Star_Games
  - https://freesound.org/s/353874/
  - CC0 1.0
- `ghost_screech_cc0.mp3`
  - **Ghost Screech** by twistedkingdom3
  - https://freesound.org/s/746112/
  - CC0 1.0
- `snow_collapse_cc0.mp3`
  - **Snow - Collapse and fall** by Vrymaa
  - https://freesound.org/s/775014/
  - CC0 1.0
- `building_collapse_cc0.mp3`
  - **R29-04-Crashing Collapse of Wood Building.wav** by craigsmith
  - https://freesound.org/s/487142/
  - CC0 1.0

The false radio and false heater remain Frozen Dawn-owned environmental cues.
They are deliberately not creature vocals.

The four Remnant radio sentences are original Frozen Dawn dialogue synthesized
from macOS system voices and transformed/layered by
`tools/generate_remnant_radio_voices.sh`. They contain no third-party recording.
