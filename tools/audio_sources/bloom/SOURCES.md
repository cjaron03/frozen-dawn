# Bloom audio sources

The Bloom drone, growth crack, frontier shimmer, Core sounds, and crystalline
layers in the Bloombound are newly processed from
`tools/audio_sources/undone_architect/ice_crack_2205.ogg`.

The Bloombound voice layers also use short, heavily transformed excerpts from
`tools/audio_sources/undone_architect/dragon_growls_cc0.ogg`. Source names,
authors, URLs, and shipping rights are retained in
`tools/audio_sources/undone_architect/SOURCES.md`.

The Bloom contact line is generated with the same local female ORSA suit-voice
workflow used by the existing interface lines. It contains no third-party
recorded performance.

The three Bloom music beds combine the processed Bloom drone with locally
synthesized low-frequency tonal layers. They contain no additional recorded
source material and are reproducible with `tools/generate_bloom_music.sh`.

The Spore's hum, footsteps, contact scrape, death impact, collapse, rooting, and corpse
fracture sounds are newly processed only from the same CC0 ice source. They are
reproducible with `tools/generate_bloom_spore_sounds.sh` and contain no vocal
or creature recordings.
