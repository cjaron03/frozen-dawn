# Frostwrithe source recordings

All source recordings below are published under Creative Commons 0 on Freesound.
The checked-in MP3 files are the high-quality Freesound previews used by the
deterministic generation script.

| Local file | Recording | Author | Source |

| `icicle_collapse_cc0.mp3` | ice icycles break on ground.flac | kyles | https://freesound.org/s/637612/ |

`tools/generate_frostwrithe_sounds.sh` trims, filters, time-stretches, layers,
and normalizes these recordings into the shipped OGG events. No vocals or
synthetic creature roars are introduced.
