# Resonant audio sources

The Resonant palette is built from physical field recordings. All four sources
are released under Creative Commons 0 and may be copied, modified, distributed,
and used commercially without attribution. Attribution is retained here for
provenance.

## Sources

- `wall_knock_cc0.mp3`: ["knocking on wall.wav" by p379](https://freesound.org/people/p379/sounds/642765/), CC0. Recorded wall knocking.
- `concrete_drag_cc0.mp3`: ["crowbar metal drag on concrete scrape and handling.wav" by kyles](https://freesound.org/people/kyles/sounds/452587/), CC0. Recorded metal dragged over concrete.
- `ice_fracture_cc0.mp3`: ["Ice cracking" by frigeriose](https://freesound.org/people/frigeriose/sounds/822369/), CC0. Frozen material recorded with a TASCAM DR-40X.
- `metal_groan_cc0.mp3`: ["Groaning Metal Hinge.wav" by sfstudios](https://freesound.org/people/sfstudios/sounds/380005/), CC0. Recorded heavy fulcrum/metal stress.
- `building_collapse_cc0.mp3`: ["building collapse / demolition" by greyfeather](https://freesound.org/people/greyfeather/sounds/712918/), CC0. Recorded/constructed building-collapse rubble.
- `stone_debris_cc0.mp3`: ["Bricks/Stones/Rocks/Gravel Falling" by iwanPlays](https://freesound.org/people/iwanPlays/sounds/567249/), CC0. Recorded falling and rolling masonry.

## Processing

`tools/generate_resonant_assets.py` cuts distinct regions from the recordings,
converts them to mono, applies restrained pitch and filtering, and layers them
by physical function. Knock, phase travel, pulse, breach, grab, injury, release,
and the two-stage death therefore have separate recorded transient vocabulary. No vocal,
choir, Thae Iven, Heart, Maeve, or synthesized-tone source is used. LISTENING
remains silent.
