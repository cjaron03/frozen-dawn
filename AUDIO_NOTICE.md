# Frozen Dawn Audio Notice

Frozen Dawn's repository-wide `LGPL-3.0-or-later` declaration applies to mod code. It does not relicense third-party recordings, generated voice performances, or source-specific derivative audio. This document is a separate asset notice and does not change the license in [LICENSE](LICENSE).

Frozen Dawn retains rights to its original audio compositions, procedural synthesis, source processing, arrangements, mixing, and implementation. Third-party recordings and derivative assets remain subject to their listed licenses and attribution requirements. Generated voice assets require separate voice-platform licensing review.

## Original and Procedural Audio

The following shipped groups are safe to identify as original Frozen Dawn procedural audio:

- All Rimebound audio generated by the checked-in deterministic synthesis scripts.
- Stillpoint Core `charge`, `form`, `hum`, `enter`, `exit`, `use`, and `exhaust` events generated from synthesized tones, filtered deterministic noise, and procedural impulses.
- Original processing scripts, arrangements, mixing decisions, implementation, and code authored for Frozen Dawn.

The generated Stillpoint ORSA voice line is not included in this procedural classification; it remains in the generated-voice review category below.

## Mixed and Source-Derived Audio

The following groups contain CC0 recordings, source-site royalty-free material, project-supplied recordings, generated voices, or combinations of those sources. Preserve each source ledger and its specific terms rather than applying a blanket Frozen Dawn all-rights-reserved claim:

- Aggregate
- Archivist
- Bloom, Bloombound, and Spore
- Frostwrithe
- Hearthrot cough, wheeze, choking, and breath layers
- Remnant
- Resonant
- Undone Architect

Detailed creators, source URLs, licenses, transformations, and deterministic processing steps are documented in [`tools/audio_sources`](tools/audio_sources). Examples include [Aggregate](tools/audio_sources/aggregate/SOURCES.md), [Remnant](tools/audio_sources/remnant/SOURCES.md), and [Resonant](tools/audio_sources/resonant/SOURCES.md).

CC0 recordings do not legally require attribution, but Frozen Dawn preserves attribution for provenance. LaSonotheque and any other source-site material remains subject to the terms recorded in its source ledger. Project-supplied recordings whose exact Creative Commons variant is not recorded must be verified before public release.

## Generated Voices

Voice assets created through macOS system voices, local TTS tooling, user-supplied generation, or other voice platforms require a separate platform-terms and distribution-rights review. Their inclusion in a deterministic processing script does not, by itself, establish ownership of the underlying generated performance.

This category includes ORSA suit/interface lines, Remnant radio lines, Stillpoint field TTS, Aggregate suit lines, Heart Successor voices, and any other generated or transformed spoken asset unless a more specific source ledger resolves it.

## Verification Required Before Release

The following groups are not granted a blanket original-audio designation by this notice and require a final source/platform review before release publication:

- Master Architect audio
- Heart and Thae Iven audio
- Undone audio
- Heart Successor voices
- `iven_battle_loop`
- Post-Maeve wind
- Surveyor Lens and UI sounds
- User-supplied or macOS-generated TTS assets

Verification should identify the source or generation platform, applicable terms on the generation/download date, allowed commercial redistribution, required attribution, and whether raw source material may remain in GitHub source archives.

## Source Archives and Distribution

GitHub source archives include raw recordings and intermediate source files under `tools/audio_sources/` so the build and provenance history remain reproducible. Those raw files are development and provenance materials; they are not packed into the Frozen Dawn mod jar unless separately copied into `src/main/resources`.

The release jar contains processed runtime assets only. Inclusion in a public Git repository or source archive does not override the source recording's license or grant downstream users rights beyond its documented terms.
