# Frozen Dawn Audio Notice

Frozen Dawn's repository-wide `LGPL-3.0-or-later` declaration applies to mod code. It does not relicense third-party recordings, generated voice performances, or source-specific derivative audio. This document is a separate asset notice and does not change the license in [LICENSE](LICENSE).

Frozen Dawn retains rights to its original audio compositions, procedural synthesis, source processing, arrangements, mixing, and implementation. Third-party recordings and derivative assets remain subject to their listed licenses and attribution requirements. Generated voice assets use the separate license treatment documented below.

## Original and Procedural Audio

The following shipped groups are safe to identify as Frozen Dawn-original or procedural audio:

- All Rimebound audio generated by the checked-in deterministic synthesis scripts.
- Architect bow releases generated by `tools/audio_sources/architect/generate_archer_audio.py` from plucked noise, filtered breath, and procedural transients.
- All Undone audio listed in `tools/audio_sources/undone/SOURCES.md`, cleared
  by the project owner's authorship declaration.
- Post-Maeve wind generated by `tools/generate_post_maeve_wind.sh`.
- O2, suit-leak, and ORSA awakening-ring cues generated by
  `tools/generate_interface_sounds.sh`.
- Stillpoint Core `charge`, `form`, `hum`, `enter`, `exit`, `use`, and `exhaust` events generated from synthesized tones, filtered deterministic noise, and procedural impulses.
- ORSA GPR-4 Plumb Line `pulse`, `scan`, `complete`, and `abort` events generated by `tools/audio_sources/plumb_line/generate_plumb_line_audio.py` from synthesized tones, filtered deterministic noise, and procedural impulses.
- ORSA AMR-1 Icarus block sounds (`blocks/icarus/*`) generated by `tools/generate_icarus_sounds.sh` from ffmpeg lavfi tone and noise synthesis with fixed seeds.
- Master Architect and Thae Iven entity/music audio listed in [their source ledger](tools/audio_sources/master_architect/SOURCES.md), cleared by the project owner's authorship declaration with no third-party or generated inputs declared.
- Original processing scripts, arrangements, mixing decisions, implementation, and code authored for Frozen Dawn.

The generated Stillpoint ORSA voice line is not included in this procedural classification; it remains in the generated-voice review category below.

## Generated Voices

The shipped synthesized speech assets use Piper's `en_US-amy-medium` model through the
checked-in local generation pipeline. The model card links to the Mycroft Mimic
3 voices dataset under CC BY-SA 4.0. Frozen Dawn preserves attribution,
identifies its modifications, and distributes the 62 processed performances
under CC BY-SA 4.0 in the [generated-voice notice](tools/audio_sources/generated_voice/NOTICE.md).
Neither the GPL-licensed Piper engine nor model weights are bundled in the jar.

This category includes ORSA suit/interface lines, Remnant radio lines, Stillpoint field TTS, Aggregate suit lines, Heart Successor voices, Master Architect telemetry speech, and any other synthesized spoken asset unless a more specific source ledger resolves it. The file-level inventory and generation paths are recorded in [the generated-voice ledger](tools/audio_sources/generated_voice/SOURCES.md).

## Source Archives and Distribution

GitHub source archives include raw recordings and intermediate source files under `tools/audio_sources/` so the build and provenance history remain reproducible. Those raw files are development and provenance materials; they are not packed into the Frozen Dawn mod jar unless separately copied into `src/main/resources`.

The release jar contains processed runtime assets only. Inclusion in a public Git repository or source archive does not override the source recording's license or grant downstream users rights beyond its documented terms.

## Recorded cyclone thundersnow

Cyclone thunder blends CC0 1.0 recordings by [cognito perceptu — thunder sharp dry crack w rumble.wav](https://freesound.org/people/cognito%20perceptu/sounds/536901/) and [loganzsound — Closeup Thunder Strike 01](https://freesound.org/people/loganzsound/sounds/840628/). Both individual pages link to [CC0 1.0 Universal](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). Frozen Dawn aligns, trims, layers, filters, gently compresses, fades, level-matches and encodes the selected recordings, including filtered tails for distance. Creator credit is voluntary under CC0. Retained public HQ MP3 previews (not WAV masters), exact URLs, rights evidence, hashes and reproducible recipes are in [the thunder source ledger](tools/audio_sources/cyclone_thunder/SOURCES.md). The owner's rejected third candidate is not used. Existing Master Architect thunder assets remain unchanged.


The expanded cyclone thunder bank also includes [big thunder clap by seth-m](https://freesound.org/people/seth-m/sounds/458015/) and [Thunder #1 by Joseph SARDIN](https://bigsoundbank.com/thunder-s2718.html), each individually listed as CC0. [BigSoundBank’s license page](https://bigsoundbank.com/licenses.html) explicitly links CC0 1.0 Universal. These two independent recordings receive trimming, filtering, gentle compression, fades and encoding for close/mid/distant variants. Voluntary credit: Additional sounds: Joseph SARDIN - BigSoundBank.com. Exact downloads, license evidence and source hashes remain in the same thunder ledger.
