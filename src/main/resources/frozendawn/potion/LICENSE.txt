table.bin and vocab.txt are minishlab/potion-base-8M, its weights rounded to int8 with a scale per row and its
WordPiece vocabulary. tools/intent_trial/ExportPotion.java writes them from the published model.

potion-base-8M is listed as MIT on its model card, https://huggingface.co/minishlab/potion-base-8M, and is built with
Model2Vec, https://github.com/MinishLab/model2vec. It is distilled from BAAI/bge-base-en-v1.5, listed as MIT on its
model card, https://huggingface.co/BAAI/bge-base-en-v1.5. The notices follow.

Training data: the weights were trained on minishlab/tokenlearn-c4-en-bge-base-v1.5, whose card states no licence.
C4 itself, allenai/c4, is ODC-BY. The project owner reviewed this on 2026-11-05 (docs/aurora-intent-trial.md).
