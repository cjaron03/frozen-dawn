# Frozen Dawn Assets

Original Frozen Dawn visual, music, and audio assets identified in this document are copyright (C) 2025-2026 Jaron Cabral. All rights reserved unless otherwise noted. These assets are not covered by the LGPL license applying to source code.

Homo Reliquus adds processed field recordings, source-site material, and generated voices that cannot be covered by a blanket original-asset declaration. Audio-specific rights, unresolved review items, and source-ledger links are documented in [AUDIO_NOTICE.md](AUDIO_NOTICE.md). Third-party and dependency notes are documented in [THIRD_PARTY_ASSETS.md](THIRD_PARTY_ASSETS.md).

## Original Branding and Interface Art

- ORSA logo, O-mark, masthead, motto treatment, boot logo, and Patchouli masthead art
- ORSA First Awakening intro art, including the masthead base and animated yellow stripe overlays
- Terminal, thermal visor, HUD, diagnostic, and ORSA boot interface presentation assets
- All GUI textures in `src/main/resources/assets/frozendawn/textures/gui/`
- All final replacement UI textures shipped under `src/main/resources/assets/minecraft/`, including HUD, container, widget, advancement, and splash presentation assets

The `assets/minecraft` files are original Frozen Dawn replacement UI textures authored for in-game compatibility. They are not copied, traced, recolored, or mechanically derived from Mojang/Microsoft texture assets.

## Original Game Textures

- All block textures in `src/main/resources/assets/frozendawn/textures/block/`
- All item textures in `src/main/resources/assets/frozendawn/textures/item/`

- All slot and miscellaneous textures in `src/main/resources/assets/frozendawn/textures/slot/` and `src/main/resources/assets/frozendawn/textures/misc/`
- Frozen terrain, sulfur, volcanic, ORSA machine, transponder, radio, supply crate, forge, fuel silo, rocket, armor, survival gear, document, and late-game material textures

## Original Map, Ending, and Release Art

## Original Homo Reliquus Procedural Audio

- All Rimebound audio generated by `tools/generate_rimebound_sounds.py` and `tools/generate_rimebound_sound_polish.py`
- Stillpoint Core charge, form, hum, enter, exit, use, and exhaust events generated by the scripts under `tools/audio_sources/stillpoint_core/`
- Frozen Dawn-authored processing scripts, procedural synthesis, arrangements, mixing, and runtime implementation

The Stillpoint generated voice line is excluded from the procedural designation. Aggregate, Archivist, Bloom/Bloombound/Spore, Frostwrithe, Hearthrot respiratory layers, Remnant, Resonant, and Undone Architect contain source-derived or mixed material and are documented in [AUDIO_NOTICE.md](AUDIO_NOTICE.md) and their checked-in source ledgers.
