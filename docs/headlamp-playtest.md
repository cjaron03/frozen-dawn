## Prototype contract

This is a lab-only aimed light with one forward line of sources, starting four blocks ahead at light 15 and fading to light 9 at 16 blocks. There is no constant source at the player's head or fan of side rays. A nearby opaque wall receives a light-15 spot before the regular four-block start, so approaching it does not reduce the source brightness. At most seven candidate sources are requested. Native block light still spreads sideways; this approximates a focused headlamp rather than rendering a sharply bounded cone. Opaque shapes stop direct projection, while glass and water transmit it. It uses the Lantern's shared marker lifecycle and maximum requested brightness; one source cannot erase a light still needed by another. Occupied terrain and foreign vanilla/alarm lights are preserved.

Press H to toggle the prototype, with free lab power. Rebind it in Controls > Frozen Dawn > Toggle Headlamp. It only responds during focused gameplay and confirms ON/OFF in the action bar. Chat buttons also activate the prototype. The inventory module, helmet requirement and 50 W cell accounting belong to slice two. The prototype starts OFF again after a world restart. A real loaded Battery Lantern is supplied separately for offhand overlap checks; it consumes its own cell normally.
