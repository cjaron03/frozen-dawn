## Approved behavior

- Regional moving front, width 1600 blocks, speed .2 blocks/tick; immutable target and bearing, including after logout.
- Persist simulation-time origin, plan, consumed index and per-storm warnings. Sleep advances scheduling, not the active timeline.
- Up to four storms, first Moderate 2-4 days into Phase 5; minimum 3 days between starts; no new starts in the final day. Late starts postpone the rest instead of accumulating a backlog.
- Catastrophic enabled by default, 5% only from third event onward; at least 60 seconds of warning at the original target. Disable downgrades planned Catastrophic to Severe.
- No starts during Master Architect fights; suppress active cyclone effects during a fight. End immediately when erased/below Phase 5; Phase 6 causes 60-second dissipation, then ends.
- Peaks: -8/-19/-30 C, visibility 8/4/2, goggles 20/12/8. Surface cold and extra heating demand are slice 2; numbers are informational in slice 1.
- EVA keeps cold/wind-chill immunity but later remains subject to push; crouching removes added drift. Roofs block direct push; upwind walls offer partial protection; insulation controls heat loss. Preserve deep-ground stability with a softened surface transition.
- Local barometer pressure/trend/severity/ETA; regional Uplink warning; personal ear-pop action bar without recurring chat spam.
- Ordinary mobile creatures receive wind effects later; bosses anchored. No new AI knowledge or forced spawns.
- Snowdrifts later: allow only air placement/existing snow growth, max one block, never erase plants, torches, containers or structures.
- Eye and vehicle handling deferred. Independent shake/flash controls in presentation slice; shake defaults off. Prefer verified CC0 thunder where helpful.
