## Observation and selection

`PLAYER_PREFERS_SWORD` is a separate positive belief. Witnessed direct sword damage supports it; a witnessed projectile or non-sword melee attack contradicts an existing sword belief. A confirmed shield block also counts as witnessed contact. Merely holding or switching an item teaches nothing. Projectile reports do not claim to know the weapon that launched them. Existing saved melee contradictions are not retroactively converted into sword evidence.

Ordinary support adds 0.20; an active scout assigned to this player adds 0.30; contradiction subtracts 0.35. Local sight, player eligibility, 48-block bounds, shared encounter deduplication, decay, provenance limits and erasure are unchanged. Masters and projections remain excluded. No negative-only sword belief is created before any positive sword observation.

The local Architect proposes the guard only from an eligible previous-encounter sword confidence of at least 0.75 and an empty offhand. The candidate's recovery cost is 1.5. Existing cheapest-recovery selection, attention admission, one spent commitment per player encounter, contradiction cooldown and two-failure deferral govern it. It competes with the other authored commitments; it is not a second simultaneous counter or an input-triggered parry. One player's evidence cannot authorize guarding another player.

## Local execution and counterplay

The Architect equips a marked vanilla shield. It equips before current-encounter confirmation, then checks visible close pressure at most once every ten ticks. It raises within 4.5 blocks for up to 35 ticks, lowers on lost sight or distance beyond six blocks, and allows at least 25 exposed ticks afterward. At later eligible decisions, a 30% choice adds another 25-tick attack window; it never skips two eligible guards in succession. These choices continue for the same locally engaged encounter, including beyond the former 400-tick limit. The same spent bet, attention slot and accumulated result remain in force. The stance expires after 600 ticks without qualifying local contact; positional holds keep their existing duration. The native five-tick shield startup remains vulnerable. While guarding it stops voluntary movement and attacks; successful full shield blocks add no horizontal or vertical knockback, including the contact that disables or breaks the shield. Hits that bypass the shield and outside forces retain ordinary physics. Exposed windows permit the ordinary utility AI to move and attack. Visible tracking turns at most ten degrees per tick.

Native frontal damage blocking and durability apply. Ordinary effective damage, including rear hits and damage during openings, immediately lowers the shield for at least 20 ticks. Exposed movement and attacks remain possible; guarding can resume inside the same commitment. Repeated hits can prolong exposure but never replace the commitment, equipment, attention slot or result. Low health, retreat and drinking lower the shield and suspend guarding. After recovery, the same shield can guard again using local sight and range. Damage and durability spent before and during recovery remain recorded. An axe or another shield-disabling contact, including an effective hit during an exposed window, ends it, imposes the vanilla-length 100-tick local disable, and cannot refund the encounter. A broken shield also ends it. Unsafe environmental conditions, encounter expiry, target loss/change, ineligible targets, attention eviction, death, removal, reload and erasure release equipment/use state. Only generated shields are removed; unrelated equipment is preserved. Generated shields are cleared before loot drops.

Real prevented damage is recorded separately from damage dealt/received. A surviving positive trade includes prevented damage. A fatal outgoing hit witnessed by this executor closes the stance and scores its accumulated trade; quiet contact expiry remains unknown. Disabling/breaking the shield or killing its executor is a failure. Silence, reload, hidden damage and unobserved interruptions are unknown. Results still affect later encounters, never the encounter supplying them. The final damage hook recognizes fatal executor damage before local defense releases the commitment.

The renderer uses synced vanilla offhand use and the native humanoid blocking pose. This pose overrides the ordinary thinking stance while the shield is raised. Client legibility is a live gate.

`tools/prepare_macs_shield_playtest.py` makes a separate closed-world copy named **MACS Sword Guard Encounter**. It preserves the completed camp's tactical memory and terrain. `/function macs_guard:setup` builds a separate arena without erasing beliefs. Four real iron-sword hits, one per encounter, teach the new belief. Training alone grants the player resistance. Each completed hit removes its QA actor and offers `/tick sprint 620t` for the empty gap. After 4/4 training, `/function macs_guard:warmup` reuses the real-hit advancement for a protected contact without changing the completed training count. Warmups naturally advance encounter cooldowns; they do not rewrite beliefs, performance or admission. Production confidence, cooldown and guard timings are never accelerated during combat.

Commands:

1. `/function macs_guard:setup`; wait for Ready.
2. `/function macs_guard:practice`; hit once with the iron sword in slot 1.
3. Click the yellow empty-gap message; wait for both Ready and Sprint completed. Repeat Practice until 4/4.
4. Run `/fd maeve dump` directly in chat to confirm four visible sword encounters before the combat round.
5. `/function macs_guard:start` creates an ordinary Architect and removes training protection. Watch its opening before attacking. Test a frontal sword contact, the exposed interval, and an axe strike (slot 2). Normal damage now staggers the same guard; test that it can raise again. An axe, break or terminal safety release still ends the whole guard. Allow it to retreat and drink, then approach again to check that it resumes with the same shield.
6. `/function macs_guard:finish`, describe the visible result, then `/fd maeve dump`. `/function macs_guard:again` starts an empty gap before another `/function macs_guard:start`.

Finish also serves as an emergency stop. The QA scene retains the Brutal preset and iron equipment of the copied camp, while holding the lethal world atmosphere at the prior phase-zero test setting. This isolates shield execution; broader survival and multiplayer balance remain live work. Axe contradictions legitimately lower sword confidence and may suppress the next guard; do not overwrite that evidence to force another demonstration.
