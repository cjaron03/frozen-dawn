Binder: use on a Core, relay or feedthrough, then on a target. Shift-use clears the clicked node's incident links and the tool selection. Clicking the selected endpoint cancels selection. Failed targets retain selection unless its source no longer exists. Selection is saved on the individual tool as a dimension-qualified position. The Core's normal empty-hand menu remains available. Hold the Binder and look at a node within six blocks, or keep a source selected, for node/link/inactive counts and electrical units.

Small relay, feedthrough and Core endpoint reach is 16 blocks. Large relays have 32 block reach. The shorter endpoint range governs an undirected link. Ordinary solid blocks do not obstruct beams. A geometric sealed boundary crossing must intersect a feedthrough on the boundary; alternatively, terminate the inside and outside links on a feedthrough placed in the wall. The full block preserves the pressure seal. An interior pillar inside one sealed room does not need a feedthrough. An unresolved or unloaded path suspends rather than erases saved links. This conservative decision is shown explicitly when authoring a link. The source endpoint can itself occupy a solid cell inside its room.

SavedData is per dimension and stores canonical undirected links. Limits: 1,024 links per dimension, 16 per node. Connectivity is cached and rebuilt on topology/loaded edge changes. Up to eight pending edges are validated per tick. Block geometry and chunk changes invalidate loaded edges; no link query loads chunks. Node destruction, replacement, explosions and command removal delete incident saved links through the real LevelChunk mutation hook. A blocked existing link remains saved and reconnects after the crossing is repaired. Breaking a linked feedthrough deletes its incident links. Server snapshots send up to 256 active nearby edges once per second, including empty snapshots; client state is replaced, cleared on logout, dimension checked and expires after three seconds without an update.

Beams use native cyan emissive ribbons. Pylon models use vanilla copper and sea lantern textures; Binder uses the vanilla brush texture. Crafting recipes are initial implementation choices pending owner tuning. Mining tags, drops and creative entries are included. Headless checks do not establish how the beams and models look to a player or prove multiplayer acceptance.
