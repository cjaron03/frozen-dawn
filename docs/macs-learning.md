## Conditional evidence

The initial conditional pattern is `PLAYER_PURSUES_WITHDRAWING_ARCHITECT`: **when an ordinary Architect withdraws, this player follows**. A real retreat, a scout's departure, attention-driven withdrawal, or the new authored withdrawal hold can begin an episode. A directive alone teaches nothing. The same Architect must actually move at least two blocks along its actual withdrawal route and continuously see the same eligible player for 100 overworld ticks. Samples run every ten ticks. The player must advance at least two blocks along that observed direction, stay within three blocks sideways, and finish with a gap no more than three blocks greater than the initial gap. A visible non-following response contradicts the hypothesis. This is a movement description, not a claim about intent or hidden health.

Both endpoints require the Architect's own sight, the same loaded dimension and an eligible Survival/Adventure player. Episodes start within 1–16 blocks; the established 48-block observation limit remains in force. Missing entities, disabled AI, death, occlusion, dimension changes, backwards time, a sampling gap over 20 ticks, a movement jump over four blocks per sample, or less than two blocks of actual withdrawal produce no belief contribution. No inventory or player-health polling occurs. Masters, mind copies and Aggregate children remain excluded.

The existing player encounter identity, per-polarity deduplication, +0.20/−0.35 scoring, 0.75 commitment threshold and 20-day stale/half-life policy apply. Four separate witnessed following responses can reach 0.80. Evidence gained in an encounter cannot unlock that encounter's counter. Provenance retains the observing executor, encounter, dimension, final player position, start time and both actors' relevant starting/ending positions. Action text is bounded to 256 characters; existing shorter saved actions remain valid.

At high confidence, `WITHDRAW_AND_HOLD` uses existing safe, level movement to step five blocks away from the currently visible player and defend that fixed position. Its thinking pose and 400-tick hold use the existing executor. Its direction comes from local sight; its prediction comes from prior encounters. It may defend itself at melee range but does not chase a broken prediction. The familiar one-bet limit, contradiction cooldown, attention admission and cheapest-safe-recovery ordering still apply. Below threshold the conditional can add up to 0.30 to local fortification utility while the Architect is already retreating.

## Counter-result memory

Each player retains at most eight `(authored pattern, dimension)` contexts. Each context records uses, successes, failures, unknown results, last use, eight retained results and an effectiveness estimate. The authored positioning families are ranged cover, recovery watch, access watch and withdrawal hold. The implementation measures the results of these holds; it does not claim to have implemented siege learning or a breach policy.

A use begins when a directive is issued. Final, effective damage is attributed only to the selected executor and subject while the executor is at its reached hold. Surviving with more witnessed damage dealt than received is a **success**; a non-positive trade with witnessed damage received is a **failure**. An observed death of the executor is also a failure. No damage, interrupted execution, reload, eviction, unload or unobserved incoming damage, hazards or another player's interference is **unknown**. A blocked arrow or a quiet hold alone cannot earn success. This is an authored combat-outcome heuristic, not a calibrated measure of whether the player felt pressured. Results retain bounded provenance and damage totals; they do not archive every hit indefinitely.

Effectiveness uses the most recent eight results with a neutral prior: `(1 + weighted successes) / (2 + weighted successes + weighted failures)`. Unknown results do not vote. Result weight starts decaying after 20 Minecraft days with a 20-day half-life. At each new encounter, Maeve freezes the result-based utility multiplier (0.5 + effectiveness). The current encounter cannot change its own inherited multiplier or cancel a bet through new performance evidence.

Two consecutive assessed failures defer that context for the next two encounters. Unknown attempts are neutral; success resets the failure streak. The deferral and the frozen multiplier survive reload. After the two encounters, a later probe can try the counter again, allowing its estimate to improve. Deferred counters cannot displace another attention concern or suppress reconnaissance as an available strong bet. Among the remaining eligible commitments, the established recovery-cost ordering applies. The dump explains `RECENT_COUNTER_FAILURES` explicitly.

These are provisional recording/selection defaults. Keep the locked confidence, encounter and hold values unchanged while calibrating this new outcome heuristic in play.

## Storage, multiplayer and bounds

Tactical SavedData is version 4. Version 1–3 worlds load with empty strategy-result memory; their beliefs and spatial observations remain readable. In-progress movement episodes are transient. A saved unfinished counter becomes unknown on load and never resumes execution or grants a second bet. Complete external backups restore their saved knowledge; debug reversal and in-world erasure start empty. Authoritative, forced and debug erasure clear the conditional windows, diagnostics, pending uses, result histories, frozen multipliers and beliefs. The permanent violation ledger is unchanged.

Per-player histories remain isolated. Multiple witnesses share the player's encounter contribution limit; executors do not receive the store. The facade stays below its enforced 300-line limit. Existing 128-player, 16-belief and eight-provenance bounds remain. Additional bounds are 32 simultaneous conditional windows, 256 retry UUIDs, eight transient window outcomes, eight result contexts per player and eight completed results per context. Context eviction uses oldest use, then pattern and dimension. Sampling uses loaded UUID lookups, not global player scans or chunk loading. Counter expiration examines at most 128 policies once per second.

## Replay handoff

`tools/prepare_macs_learning_playtest.py` makes a separate **MACS Learning Encounter** from a closed QA world. Initial setup resets only that disposable copy. The old Slice 5 and 6 worlds remain available. All generated functions are compiled again at integrated-client permission level 2 in the required GameTests.

1. Open **MACS Learning Encounter** and run `/function macs_learning:setup`.
2. Wait on blue for the green walking prompt. Walk through the doorway to gold. Click the empty-gap sprint and wait for **Sprint completed** and **Ready**. Start the scout with the green link or `/function macs_learning:start`.
3. Let it inspect first. When it turns away after thinking, follow in open sight for five seconds. At the scripted pause, describe what you saw before `/fd maeve dump`. The dump must show a witnessed following contribution. `/function macs_learning:next` prepares the next entrance. Four separate rounds build the conditional history; entering/exiting alternates to avoid training an unrelated strong exit bearing.
4. After the fourth verified response, Next prepares the counter gap. Sprint that empty gap, then `/function macs_learning:counter`. If it steps away, follow enough to preserve the observed tendency, stay around five blocks away, and land a bow hit during its hold. Keep it alive. The round pauses after 26 seconds so a completed hold can be scored.
5. Describe, dump, then `/function macs_learning:repeat` for another encounter using the same memory. After two assessed failures, compare the next encounter: its choice should change, with the dump attributing the deferral to those prior uses. Avoid assuming a result from a scripted round number; inspect the actual retained events.

Use `/function macs_learning:status` for stage counters. `/function macs_learning:stop` safely disables the actor and exports its trace; the interrupted use is inconclusive. `/function macs_learning:counter_gap` starts a later attempt without erasing memory. Only empty, actor-free encounter gaps use `/tick sprint 620t`; production timing, confidence and damage remain unchanged. Dumps must run directly in chat because function feedback can suppress their text.

The live record must show the first impressions, relevant dumps, actor traces, actual counter outcomes and the later changed choice. It should also include deliberately staying put or breaking sight to distinguish a witnessed wrong prediction from unknown evidence. Ranged, recovery, access and attention features remain enabled in the integrated build; their earlier isolated fixtures remain useful for regression. A single replay is not proof of every feature combination, multiplayer pressure or final horror calibration.
