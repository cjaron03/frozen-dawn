# Pressure-preserving heat vent

Source: Notion Thermal Model and Heater Control, including Additions (2026-10-09), fetched October10,2026. The source specifies heat rejection without pressure loss; power, rate, recipe and placement were open. Owner selected this supporting block next, ahead of the thermometer.

First-pass implementation: an insulated full-collision wall/roof block. FACING points outdoors; the opposite adjacent cell must belong to a known sealed room. Right-click toggles a saved manual shutter. Redstone holds it open, without overwriting the manual setting. An exposed outlet must be loaded, passable and at/above the motion-blocking heightmap. Glass roofs, overhangs, buried outlets and neighboring rooms do not qualify. No chunk loading, neighbor-room energy sink or gas transfer. Open loaded block entities renew the existing bounded pressure activity lease.

The exchanger adds8 game conductance units while active (2.8 in vacuum through the existing0.35 radiative multiplier). It removes heat from the room air; with depleted gas it removes structural energy. Each existing solver substep uses exact finite-reservoir loss toward the exterior background at the outlet Y, clamped to cooling only. It never crosses the cold-side temperature in its own transfer, cannot act as a refrigerator, and records every removed unit in environmentLoss. Existing wall cooling remains independently active. Both shutter states retain the same insulated housing conductance0.1, heat capacity and pressure geometry. Several vents add conductance; larger thermal reservoirs take longer to cool. Balance is provisional.

Heaters keep their current targets and can burn more fuel to oppose an open vent. The thermostat controls heaters only; vent shutters are manual/redstone. Core warmth remains an additive local sensing term, not an energy source in the room solver. No proposed hyperthermia thresholds are changed here.

Recipe: six iron ingots, two copper ingots and one lever, ICI/ILI/ICI. Pickaxe harvest; native shaped recipe for JEI and Patchouli. The manual distinguishes this sealed exchanger from the oxygen-dumping manual vent valve.

Physics reference: NASA Roman's Radiators and Thermal System, https://roman.gsfc.nasa.gov/interactive/subparts/wfi-thermal-system/ . Used for the principle of carrying heat to an exterior radiator, not numerical game constants or copied code. This is a deliberately simplified game model, not a Stefan-Boltzmann simulation.

Model provenance: heat_vent_closed.json and heat_vent_open.json are original native cuboid models authored in this patch. They reference the existing Frozen Dawn airlock_casing and airlock_controller_green textures, and the Minecraft runtime iron_block texture. No downloaded textures, third-party models, sounds or dependencies are incorporated. Shutter feedback uses the existing vanilla iron-trapdoor sound. No texture rights are inferred from an external mod.

Replay: owning Java21 Gradle runClientLab, fresh Heat Vent Check from seed metadata only. Two matching wool rooms, identical Core supplies, heaters and30C thermostats; both vents closed initially. Fuel both, wait120 normal seconds, heaters off, manually open only the left vent. Compare30/60-second cooling; oxygen, lit torches and seal must remain intact. Check blockage and redstone only after the first comparison. Generated functions guard the fixture; no bridge mutation whitelist is added. Saved-data reconstruction/native tests are separate from actual world reopen, human visual or multiplayer acceptance.

## ORSA service grille

The room-facing surface now has a recessed dark grille, five metal slats, four
corner bolts, a shutter slider and a status light. These are original native cuboid
models generated by tools/build_heat_vent_models.py, referencing the existing owned
airlock casing and vanilla iron/black/gray/yellow/lime concrete materials. The solid
pressure collision is retained. Every facing, manual shutter/redstone combination
and indicator state has an explicit model variant.

Light, sampled once per second: dim = closed; green = an exposed qualifying outlet
and a room reservoir warmer than outdoors; amber = open but waiting, obstructed,
wrongly oriented or not bound to an active sealed room. A warm exterior also means
amber. No new thermal transfer or geometry inspection is introduced by the light.
The manual explains these states. Owner accepted the service grille and machinery replay on October 10, 2026 with “ok it works.” This is local visual acceptance; actual guest-client playback remains unverified.
