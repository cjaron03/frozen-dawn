# The Descent Chambers Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task, inline. The owner avoids subagents, except the one branch review at the end. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Build slice 2 of the descent (CH-44 to CH-47): the chambers themselves, stacked top to bottom and placed by a dev command into a test world. This slice adds:
- the hidden-division site, with its seated figure and the 2077 slot;
- the 1816 house and the eight era chambers, each holding its slice 1 instrument, mark and motif;
- the crawl down to chamber zero, with its drip and its advancement, `To You, 200,000 Years Ago`.

Nothing generates in a world yet: that is slice 3.

**Architecture:**
- **Templates.** One structure template per piece, generated by a Python script from readable layouts, so they are reproducible and reviewable.
- **Placement.** A pure layout stacks the pieces, and a placer puts them in a world.
- **New blocks.** Three, each small and purpose-built:
  - the seated figure;
  - the 2077 slot, whose block entity draws the CH-18 line;
  - chamber zero's drip, whose block entity plays the icicle clip while someone is near and grants the advancement.

**Tech Stack:** Java 21, NeoForge 1.21.1, Python 3 for the template generator, JUnit in `src/test`, GameTests in `src/gameTest`.

**Spec:** [the-descent-plan.md](the-descent-plan.md) (slice 1 and the slice outline), [the-descent-checkpoint.md](the-descent-checkpoint.md), [payoff-roadmap.md](payoff-roadmap.md) branch 9. ## Global Constraints

- **Text.** The only text in the whole descent is the 2077 slot's line, `screen.frozendawn.payoff.death_line.2` (`1 enrollee remaining.`), reused word for word. Everything else carries none:
  - the seated figure's badge is ten blocked characters drawn as bars in its texture, never glyphs;
  - no sign, lectern, book, map, name tag, banner or custom-named item anywhere in a template;
  - the advancement's text lives only in its own lang keys.
- **The count** (CH-44). Top to bottom, the pieces show:

  | Piece | Count |
  |---|---|
  | The site | 1, the CH-18 line |
  | The house (`HOUSE_1816`) | 2 standing |
  | `MAUNDER` to `GLACIAL_MAXIMUM` | 3 to 10 standing |
  | Chamber zero | none: the scratches, one figure |

  Each era chamber holds exactly its own era's instrument, mark and motif, and no other era's.
- **No presence** (CH-14, CH-32). No light source in a chamber reads as a watcher, no figure stands in the dark, and no chamber has anything "on the other side". Light is period-plain: candles, embers, lamps, or none.
- **Chamber zero is nobody's** (CH-32, CH-45). No bed, respawn anchor, player head, armor stand, chest, sign or item of the player's. It has no instrument and no Echo.
- **No world generation** in this slice. The descent appears only through the dev command.
- **Rules for every branch.** Never edit MACS. Never open the Notion page "SEALED: Sequel Seed". Cite canon by ID, and keep Notion-only notes out of code, comments, commits and the PR. New Java names, keys and files use "descent", never "continuity" or "archivist".

## Rulings

1. **The pieces.** A new pure class, `descent/DescentLayout`, lists the pieces top to bottom:
   1. `site`
   2. `house_1816`
   3. one piece per era from `maunder` to `glacial_maximum`
   4. `crawl`
   5. `chamber_zero`

   Each piece records its template id (`frozendawn:descent/<piece>`), its size, and the local positions of its entry and its exit. A piece's entry lies directly under the exit of the piece above it, so the stack is one walkable route down. Sizes and positions come from the approved mockup.
2. **The templates.** `tools/generate_descent_templates.py` writes every `data/frozendawn/structure/descent/*.nbt` from one layout table per piece.
   - **Determinism.** The output is the same on every run. `--check` exits non-zero when a committed file is stale.
   - **Blocks and entities.** Materials are vanilla blocks plus the slice 1 and slice 2 blocks. Each era chamber's motif is a painting entity with its `descent/<era>` variant. Instruments are saved with `playing=false`.
   - **Its own code.** The script brings its own small NBT writer and does not edit or import another feature's tools.
3. **The site.** Bare light-gray concrete with no logos, posters, ORSA IDs or lights left on.
   - One `descent_seated_figure` sits at a desk facing inward, toward the 2077 slot on the far wall.
   - The way down is a plain concrete stair to the house.
4. **The seated figure.** `DescentSeatedFigureBlock`, two blocks tall (lower and upper halves, as a door is), with a horizontal facing and no block entity.
   - It shows a frozen, frosted figure in a 2070s jumpsuit, seated, its head bowed. The badge on its chest is ten black bars.
   - It drops nothing, and a pick block gives nothing.
   - In the creative tab it is `Frozen Figure`.
5. **The 2077 slot.** `DescentSlotBlock` with `DescentSlotBlockEntity`, a flat panel on the wall.
   - **What it draws.** A block entity renderer draws the line `screen.frozendawn.payoff.death_line.2` on its face, in the CH-18 death line's color, with no frame, header or date.
   - **The rule.** It is pure: `DescentSlot.shows(boolean maeveEscaped)` returns `!maeveEscaped`.
   - **Sync.** On load, and every 100 ticks, the block entity sets its synced `empty` flag from `PayoffState.maeveEscaped(server)`. It sends a block update only when that flag changes.
   - **Empty.** When `empty` is set, the panel draws as bare panel.
   - **In the creative tab** it is `Panel`.
6. **The house and the chambers.**
   - **The house.** A timber room whose walls carry `descent/house_1816` (the sampler) and `descent/portrait_1816`, with the music box on a table. Its mark is the 1816 ink.
   - **Each era chamber.** It is built in its era's materials as the slice 1 mark materials set them. It holds:
     - its instrument, on the floor or a plinth, within reach;
     - its mark, set in the wall at eye height;
     - its motif, on the wall the player faces on arrival.
   - **The cave.** `glacial_maximum` is the cave, with no built walls.
   - **Fixed at mockup.** Lighting and dressing are set in the mockup and kept by Global Constraints.
7. **Chamber zero.**
   - **The way in.** `crawl` is a passage one block wide and two blocks tall, so a player fits without tricks. It runs about 12 blocks down and along from the cave floor.
   - **The cave itself.** `chamber_zero` is a small sea cave:
     - a frozen shoreline of packed and blue ice;
     - a midden of bone blocks and gravel;
     - an unlit campfire for the cold hearth's ash;
     - red terracotta for the ochre lump, carrying `descent/chamber_zero_scratches`.
   - **The crack.** A crack one block wide runs down through the floor into blue ice and closes after four blocks. Nothing is below it.
   - **The drip.** One `descent_drip` hangs from the ceiling over the stones.
8. **The drip and the advancement.** `DescentDripBlock` with `DescentDripBlockEntity`, shaped as a pointed icicle. It drops nothing, and `/fd debug descent place` is the only way to place one.
   - **The loop.** Each second the block entity checks for a non-spectator player within 8 blocks.
     - If one is there and no clip is running, it plays `ambient.descent.icicle` at the drip and stays quiet for `CLIP_TICKS` (the icicle clip's length).
     - With no one near, it plays nothing.
   - **The advancement.** In the same check, a player within 6 blocks is granted `to_you_200000_years_ago` through `WorldTickHandler.grantAdvancement`. Vanilla grants it only once.
   - **Its JSON.** `advancement/to_you_200000_years_ago.json`:
     - `minecraft:impossible` criterion, parent `frozendawn:root`;
     - frame `goal` (no challenge fanfare in chamber zero's quiet);
     - `show_toast` true, `announce_to_chat` false, `hidden` true;
     - icon `minecraft:pointed_dripstone`.
   - **Lang.** Title `To You, 200,000 Years Ago`, description `Nothing new under the sun. (Ecclesiastes 1:9)`.
   - **Never on a death.** Nothing about the CH-18 death grants it.
9. **Slice 1's minors.** Each is fixed with a failing test first:
   - **Rotation.** `DescentInstrumentBlock`, `DescentSeatedFigureBlock` and `DescentSlotBlock` override `rotate` and `mirror`, so slice 3 can turn a template.
   - **A stuck instrument.** An instrument saved as `playing=true` with no scheduled tick resets to `playing=false` on its first random or neighbour update.
   - **The cooldown.** It gains a one-tick margin.
   - **The playtest comment.** The one that contradicts its chat labels is corrected.
10. **Dev commands.** Development builds only, beside slice 1's `/fd debug descent play`:
    - `/fd debug descent place`: places the whole stack with the site's floor at the caller's feet, going down. It clears what it replaces and prints the top and bottom coordinates.
    - `/fd debug descent goto <piece>`: moves the caller to that piece's entry in the last stack placed this session.

## Review Focus

1. **A piece out of order.** One era's things in another's chamber, or a count that skips, breaks the descent. Task 2's GameTest walks the placed stack and checks each chamber's instrument, mark and painting against `DescentEra`.
2. **Text that slipped in.** A sign, book, name or glyph in any template breaks CH-44. Task 1's GameTest scans every template for text-bearing blocks, entities and items. The owner checks the textures.
3. **A blocked route.** Any piece whose exit does not meet the next entry strands the player. Task 2's GameTest checks a two-high air gap at every joint.
4. **The slot in an E11 world.** The slot must empty when Maeve has escaped and show the line otherwise. Task 3's unit test pins `DescentSlot.shows`, and its GameTest pins the sync flag.
5. **The drip.** It must never stack its clip, never play with no one near, and never grant the advancement outside chamber zero. Task 4's GameTests hold all three.
