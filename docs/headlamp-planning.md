## All six recommendations accepted by the owner

1. Beam: recommend a narrow forward cone with light 14 near the wearer and projected sources reaching about 12 blocks, weakening with distance. Ordinary Minecraft light spill remains; direct projection stops at opaque obstacles. Confirm this starting target rather than a broad Lantern-like glow.
2. Draw: recommend 50 W while enabled and equipped, zero off/offline draw, independent of O2. One 500 kJ cell funds 10,000 active seconds (2 h 46 min 40 s). If an offhand Lantern is also on, both devices pay their own draw.
3. Cell choice: recommend loose inventory cells sequentially, hotbar first and then inventory order, automatically continuing to the next charged cell and resuming when charge is supplied. Never draw from a cell inside a Lantern or other container/device.
4. Controls: recommend a rebindable H toggle, initially OFF, with the module's switch setting saved across reload. Restore the same setting after cell replacement; helmet removal stops light/drain without changing the saved switch.
5. Compatibility: recommend the standard EVA Helmet and ORSA Thermal Visor; require the helmet, not a complete suit. Other helmets, including temporary Emergency EVA equipment, remain unsupported for the first version.
6. Acquisition: recommend an ordinary iron/glass/copper/redstone recipe; the compatible helmet provides progression gating, with no additional rare salvage component. Exact nonconflicting pattern is selected during the recipe slice.

The owner accepted all six recommendations on October 18. Slice one implementation and verification are in progress. Existing lighting saves and the prior client remain in the lighting checkout. Slice two and three remain deferred until owner acceptance of the current slice.
