# Frozen Dawn 2.0.1 Alpha — Stability Update

This update gives The Long Silence a more reliable foundation. The biggest change is the Architect: it handles awkward terrain, interrupted building, and multiplayer chases much more consistently. There are also fixes for world update stalls, obstructed Hearths, and visual effects.

## The Architect is much harder to trip up

The Architect should now spend more time finding its way to you and less time getting caught on the terrain. Its pauses to think are still there, including a new hand-to-chin pose. Those pauses are part of its character; the fixes focus on getting it moving again when something actually goes wrong.

- Fixed cases where it mined the floor it needed to stand on. It makes safer choices around bridges, narrow ledges, and changes in height.
- Improved turns on slab bridges, stairs, mixed-height paths, fences, and gates. Open gates are treated as passages, and closed gates can be opened during pursuit.
- Fixed interrupted scaffold climbs and the descent afterward. It can recover when a target moves away, comes back, or drops to ground level, with safer checks before removing its own support blocks.
- Fixed the multiplayer bug where reconnecting could make it jump back to an old scaffold. Abandoned building steps are now canceled, and a lift must still be directly above the Architect before it can complete.
- Improved recovery when a route becomes blocked, a block changes, or a better walking route opens while it is mining. It can stop outdated work and reconsider its route.
- Fixed cases where it stopped chasing just outside melee range.
- Bounded its path search so difficult routes cannot keep expanding without a limit.

## More consistent multiplayer behavior

- Architects commit to a player instead of constantly switching as players trade places. Roaming Architects can still prioritize a much more vulnerable player.
- Losing a target through death, disconnecting, or a switch to Creative or Spectator allows another eligible player to take over.
- Fixed an interaction where an unreachable player on a retry cooldown could prevent the Architect from choosing someone else.
- Hearth Architects coordinate their assessments so both do not keep selecting the same player. Creative players are excluded from those assessments.
- Corrected player lookup for Hearth transmissions to use the player's current level.

## World, Hearth, and visual fixes

- World catch-up now resumes unfinished column scans instead of repeatedly starting them over. This reduces wasted work when older chunks need to catch up with the freeze.
- Landmark planning has a time budget per tick to reduce long stalls during planning.
- Hearth scenes that were only partly placed because something was in the way can repair themselves when the obstruction clears.
- Fixed depth handling for screen-space effects, including Fabulous graphics, and restored blending correctly after the eye-wall effect.
- Smoothed camera shake between game ticks and corrected the empty boots-slot icon.

## Better tools for catching the next bug

There is now an optional visual debugger that shows the Architect's route, movement, mining, scaffold work, and decisions. Its observations can also be saved in logs and dumps, so a strange moment can be investigated afterward. It uses Minecraft's own debug rendering helpers and adds no new rendering dependency. Recording is off unless enabled.

The test labs now include ordinary terrain, wilderness routes, accumulating snow and freezing, construction, broken routes, and interrupted scaffolding. We also fixed lab problems that could make a successful chase look like an Architect failure, including snow-blocked test villagers, unusable detours, course completion, and gate-reopening checks.

Save-and-reload coverage now exercises more persistent world and encounter state. The automated checks also reject missing or skipped required results, so a test server that never ran its tests cannot count as a pass.

## What we tested

The combined build passed **535 unit tests, 67 GameTests, and all 500 seeded stress cases**. Both final two-player LAN replays passed: disconnecting and reconnecting during scaffolding, and disconnecting while another Survival player remained. The Architect recovered from its current position, switched targets when appropriate, and continued pursuit without the old remote scaffold jump.

Earlier live replays also exercised slab bridges, reopening gates, scaffold interruptions, unloading, target replacement, tunnels, and changing routes. This is still an alpha: those results give us much stronger confidence, but they do not mean every possible world or mod combination has been tested. A full uninterrupted 20–30-minute soak is not claimed, and a few earlier snowy-shelter observations lacked complete recordings.

## Installing or updating

Download **`frozendawn-2.0.1-alpha.jar`** below and replace the old Frozen Dawn jar. Restart Minecraft and update the server as well if you play multiplayer. Existing worlds are supported; back up your save before updating.

Requires **Minecraft 1.21.1**, **Java 21**, and **NeoForge 21.1.219 or newer**, with Patchouli 1.21.1-90+, Curios 9+, and GeckoLib 4.8.4 or a compatible newer 4.x version. JEI 19+ is required on the client. Keep those dependency jars installed.

The attached `SHA256SUMS.txt` lets you verify the download. Audio notices and the shipped audio inventory are included in the jar.
