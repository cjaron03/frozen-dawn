The other four ideas

Same frosted silo. Click any you want to keep (pick as many as you like).

1

Frost spreads on the floor

In deep cold a ring of frost creeps out across the ground around the base; when it warms, the frost shrinks back. It loops here from warm to cold and back.

2

Any height, one bar per block

Build it 4, 6 or 8 blocks tall (any height in between too). Each block holds more charge and adds a bar. All three here are about 60% full.

3

Scorched after a quench

Burnt streaks run down from the vent and the bar slot, the bars sit dead grey with a faint ember glow, and the pipes stay bare and wet. It all clears once the silo is cold again.

4

Idle cold breath

Every few seconds while it's healthy and cold, a small puff of frosty breath curls out of the vent, so it feels alive even when nothing is happening.
