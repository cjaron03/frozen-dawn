The Energy Core trick: it stops looking like blocks

What makes that core feel big is that once it's built it becomes one glowing, moving object, and its motion follows what it's doing. Three ways to give the silo that, in our own style (no orb copy). They can be combined; click any you like.

A

Seams vanish, a living column inside

Once formed, the block grid melts into one smooth rounded tank. The bar slot becomes a tall window onto a glowing column of charge that swirls upward; dark rungs across it keep the stacked-bar read. Faster swirl when charging, slower when draining.

B

Floating magnet rings

Two heavy steel rings levitate around the silo, bobbing and spinning. Idle: slow spin. Charging: fast spin with a blue glow. Overheating: they wobble and tilt. Quench: they drop and clang onto the base (the puck idea, scaled up and outside where you can see it).

C

Arcs to the ports

When a lot of power moves, crackling blue arcs jump from a coil on top of the silo to the port blocks wired to it, the busier the more often. Quiet at a trickle, a light show at full flow.
