Ring style: which one looks best?

Close-up of the rings around the silo. In game they'd be built from little blocky boxes so they match Minecraft's pixel look; here they're smoothed so you can judge the idea.

1

Segmented ring

Eight separate floating steel segments with a glowing seam, each bobbing a little out of step so a ripple runs round the ring. When it overheats the segments drift apart and jitter; on quench they scatter outward, then fall and clatter onto the base.

2

Wound magnet coil

A solid ring wrapped in copper windings, glowing blue on its inner face toward the silo. Reads as a real electromagnet. Spins as one piece; tilts and wobbles when overheating; drops in one heavy slam on quench.

3

Running-light band

A clean solid band with a strip of lights chasing round it. The chase speed shows the flow: fast when charging, reversed when draining, amber when hot. Sleekest, least mechanical.
