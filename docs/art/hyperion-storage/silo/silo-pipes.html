C, dressed up: which base feels right?

All three keep the stacked bars lighting bottom to top and the vent on top. What changes is the pipework and trim.

1

Heavy industrial

Steel bands every two blocks and a wide steel footing. Two fat pipes come out low on each side and bend down into the floor, with a pulse of blue moving through them while it charges.

2

Frosted cryo lines

Thick insulated pipes caked in frost run out of the base, with cold vapour rolling off them and sinking. A ring of small status lamps sits round the bottom.

3

Lab manifold

A cluster of thin copper pipes fans out of one side of the base into a valve box, and a thin coolant line climbs the back corner to the vent. Busier, more hand-built.
