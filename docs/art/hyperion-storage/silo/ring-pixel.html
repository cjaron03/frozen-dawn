Why the last rings looked off, and a fix

They were too wide, too thick and too bright: big pale steel plates around a black block tower, drawn smooth instead of in pixels. These are drawn at Minecraft's 16 pixels per block, slimmer, closer to the silo, and mostly lit by their glow.

1

Square collars

Thin square bands in the same black material, hugging the silo a few pixels out, with one glowing line. Follows the silo's square shape.

2

Light halos

No metal at all: floating rings of pure blue light, pulsing out of step. Lightest touch; reads as energy, not machinery.

3

Thin dark rings

Slim round rings in black metal with a glowing inner edge. Still physical, so they can fall and clang on quench.
