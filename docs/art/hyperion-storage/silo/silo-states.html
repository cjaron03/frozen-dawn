Ideas: the frosted silo telling you what it's doing

Option 2 in five moments. The bars, pipes, lamps and vent all change with what the battery is doing, so you can read it from across the base without opening the screen.

Cold and full

All bars lit, a slow shimmer. Frosted pipes shed vapour that sinks. Lamps steady blue.

Charging

Blue pulses flow in through the pipes. The next bar up breathes on and off until it fills.

Discharging

Pulses flow out. The top lit bar flickers as it drains, then goes dark.

Overheating

Bars turn amber and blink. Frost melts off the pipes and drips. Wisps rise from the vent.

Quench

Bars flash red and collapse top to bottom. The vent blasts steam, as it does now.

Other ideas, not drawn yet

  • Frost spreads on the floor around the base while it's deep cold, and melts back when it warms.
  • Any height: build it 4 to 8 blocks tall, with one bar per block. Taller means more storage and more bars.
  • Scorched after a quench: dark streaks down the silo and dead bars until it's cold again (like the scars now).
  • Idle cold breath: a thin wisp curls out of the vent every few seconds while it's happy.
