# Frostwrithe audio

Frostwrithe uses only processed physical recordings: real insect movement,
ice grinding, organic frozen cracking, and structural ice collapse. This keeps
the colony non-vocal and makes its sound follow the same physical rule as its
body: many small movements held together by rime.

All five source recordings are CC0. Full URLs, authors, local source names, and
the generation workflow are recorded in
`tools/audio_sources/frostwrithe/SOURCES.md`.

Generated events:

- `movement`: close dry scurrying under filtered ice grit.
- `assemble` / `regroup`: converging scurry followed by a tightening freeze.
- `shell`: compacting organic ice with the insect bed sharply reduced.
- `shed`: a short plate crack and one abrupt scuttle.
- `disassemble`: a hard collapse opening into a widening swarm.
- `body_check`, `climb`, `bridge`, and `overrun`: short physical variants of
  the same source vocabulary, never a roar or voice.
- `terminal`: the ice lattice fails and the remaining representatives lose
  colony ownership without adding a vocal death cue.
